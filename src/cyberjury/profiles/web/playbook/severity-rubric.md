# Severity Rubric

Grade an evidenced exploit by its reachable impact and required attacker preconditions.

- **CRITICAL**: broad compromise of protected authority, assets, or confidential data
  with few realistic preconditions.
- **HIGH**: serious unauthorized effect or sensitive disclosure with a practical path.
- **MEDIUM**: a real but bounded effect or a path with substantial preconditions.
- **LOW**: a narrow real exploit with limited effect.

Do not use severity to dismiss a real finding.
